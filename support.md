# Pawlaris Support

For questions, feedback or bug reports, email us at **[pawlaris@sentiralabs.app](mailto:pawlaris@sentiralabs.app)**

If you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version and a screenshot of the problem. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.

## FAQ

### How do the 5 free full analyses work?

Each Apple ID gets 5 free full food analyses. One is used only when you photograph an ingredient label and the analysis completes successfully. Retakes for unclear photos, failed analyses and searches by brand or product name don't count. Deleting and reinstalling the app or switching phones doesn't reset the count.

### What can I do after the 5 analyses are used?

Allergen alerts stay free: every scan still shows whether the food contains the allergens you've set for your pet. Ingredient safety flags, nutrition scores, plain-language explanations, health-related ingredient notes and food comparison require Pawlaris Pro. Once you subscribe, scans made after your free analyses ran out unlock their full results automatically.

### The app says my photo is unclear. What should I do?

Shoot in good light, straight on, with the full ingredient list and nutrition panel sharp and in frame. Avoid glare and blur. Barcode scanning isn't supported yet, so please photograph the ingredient label.

### How many photo scans can I do per day?

To keep the service reliable, each Apple ID can do up to 20 photo scans per day (Taiwan time). You can scan again the next day. Search and scan history aren't affected.

### Why don't I see health-related ingredient notes?

Health-related ingredient notes currently cover some conditions, including kidney, joint, weight management, skin and digestive concerns, and only appear when the food contains a relevant ingredient. They're for reference only, not medical advice.

### Can I change my pet's allergens or health conditions?

In the current version, allergens and health conditions are set when you first create your pet's profile and can't be changed afterwards yet. Email us if you need help.

### Where is my scan history stored? What happens if I delete the app?

Pet profiles and scan history are stored only on your iPhone and aren't synced to the cloud. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently. You can also swipe left on a record in the History tab to delete it.

## Subscriptions

### How do I restore my purchase?

After switching phones or reinstalling, go to Settings → Pawlaris Pro → Restore Purchases in the app, signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In the app, go to Settings → Pawlaris Pro → Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pawlaris to change plans or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal.

### How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Important

Pawlaris provides educational pet nutrition information only. It does not provide veterinary diagnoses, prescriptions, or guarantees of food safety. Always consult your veterinarian about your pet's dietary and health needs.
